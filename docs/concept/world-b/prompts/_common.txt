Style: stylized low-poly 3D game world (simple faceted shapes like Kenney or Quaternius assets: chunky rounded low-poly trees, blocky logs, simple rocks) rendered with a soft hand-painted watercolor post-process — paper grain, pigment blooms, soft color bleeding at edges — and clearly visible thin dark sepia ink outlines drawn around every shape and every facet edge, like a pen-and-ink storybook illustration. Muted warm palette: olive and sage greens, ochre and sand dirt, warm umber wood, dusty blue shadows, nothing saturated. Camera: isometric orthographic game camera, looking down at about 35 degrees, rotated 45 degrees, no perspective distortion, no horizon, no sky — we only see the ground plane from above. Characters are flat hand-drawn 2D sprites standing upright on top of the 3D world (billboards), drawn in ink and watercolor, each with a small soft contact shadow.
The heroine: a young woman in her early twenties from our modern world — plain white t-shirt, rolled-up blue jeans, worn plain sneakers with no logos, small olive backpack, messy dark shoulder-length hair. She is SMALL in the frame, seen from far above like a game character: her full height is only about one ninth of the image height (a tiny figure, the world around her dominates). Every character is drawn at this same small scale.
Strictly no UI, no HUD, no health bars, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no frame border.
